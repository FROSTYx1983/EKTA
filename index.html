College profile

M.A.M.O. College, Mukkam, is a NAAC ‘A’ Grade institution affiliated to the University of Calicut. Managed by the Mukkam Muslim Orphanage Trust, it has been empowering young people through education since 1982.

Rules and regulations

General guidelines for every participant at EKTA’26.

  1. Register for each event through the official EKTA’26 registration form.
  2. Carry a valid college ID card, or a letter from your institution, for verification.
  3. Every member of a team must be from the same college.
  4. Wear the ID tag issued by the organizers for the whole fest.
  5. Reach each event venue at least 15 minutes before it starts.
  6. Follow the schedule and the instructions of coordinators and volunteers.
  7. Cheating, harassment, abusive language or any unethical behavior is not tolerated and can lead to disqualification.
  8. Treat fellow participants, judges, volunteers, organizers and the host college with respect.
  9. You are responsible for your own belongings and any equipment your event needs.
  10. The organizers are not responsible for lost belongings or faults in personal devices.
  11. Each event has its own rules, and participants must follow them.
  12. The organizing committee may change schedules, venues or arrangements when needed.
  13. An event may be changed or cancelled because of unforeseen circumstances or too few entries.
  14. The decisions of the judges and the organizing committee are final.
  15. The organizers may disqualify any participant or team that breaks these guidelines.
  16. Certificates and prizes will be given as per the policy announced by the organizing committee.
  17. The organizers may photograph and record the fest for promotion and documentation.
  18. Any matter not covered here will be settled by the organizing committee, and its decision is final.

By taking part, you accept these guidelines and the rules of each event you register for.
